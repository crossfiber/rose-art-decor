Christmas tree decorating in Miami Decoración de árboles de Navidad en Miami

A tree that looks designed rather than decorated comes down to two things: a color story decided before anything is bought, and layers built in the right order. That is the whole job, and it happens in your living room, not in a workshop.

The color story comes first

Most trees go wrong at the shop, not at the tree. People buy what looks nice individually, bring it home, and end up with six unrelated ideas hanging on one tree. So the palette gets written down first: two or three colors, one metal, one texture, and a rule for what does not go on.

Once that exists, everything else is easy. Red and gold reads warm and traditional. White and champagne reads quiet and expensive. Blue and silver reads cold and modern, which in Miami is its own kind of joke worth making.

Layers, in this order

Ribbon goes on first, worked into the branches rather than draped over them, so it reads as part of the tree instead of a wrapper. Then the large florals and oversized pieces, placed in clusters that anchor each section. Then the mid size ornaments filling between the clusters, then the small glass and picks catching whatever light is in the room.

The topper and the base are decided at the same time as the tree, not afterwards. A tree with a beautiful crown and a bare floor underneath always looks unfinished.

All the way around, or only what you can see

If your tree stands in the open, it gets finished on every side. If it sits against a wall or in a corner, we decorate only the exposed sides. It looks identical from the room and it costs less, and it is one of the questions on the quote form for exactly that reason.

Tree height is the other lever. A 12 foot tree is not twice the work of a 6 foot tree, it is more, because everything above about 8 feet involves a ladder and a second pair of hands.

How the day works

You answer six questions once Contestas seis preguntas una sola vez

Then the next time you walk into that room, the tree is finished. You are not managing a project.

01

Tell us about the tree

Height in feet, which room it lives in, whether it stands in the open or against a wall, and the colors you keep coming back to.

02

We write the color story

Before anything is bought, you get the palette and a written estimate: which ribbons, florals, picks and topper, and how they layer up the tree.

03

Yours, or ours for $50

Already own your decor? We design around it. If not, we shop the palette for a flat $50 plus what the materials cost, and it all stays yours.

04

We build it in your room

The three of us come to you and build it in place, all the way around, or only the exposed sides if the tree sits against a wall.
