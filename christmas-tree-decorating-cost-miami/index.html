What Christmas tree decorating costs in Miami Cuánto cuesta decorar un árbol de Navidad en Miami

Nobody can quote a tree they have not seen, and anyone giving you a flat price before knowing the height is guessing. What can be published is the logic, so you can work out roughly where you land before you send anything.

The four things that set your number

Tree height, in feet, is the biggest single factor, because everything above roughly eight feet needs a ladder and a second person. Design detail is the second: a tree with three ribbon types, layered florals and a built up base is a different job to a clean two color tree.

Third is whether it is finished all the way around or only on the exposed sides, which is a real saving on any tree that sits against a wall. Fourth is whether we are sourcing the materials, which adds a flat $50 plus whatever the materials actually cost.

The $50 line, explained

If you would rather not shop, we do it for a flat $50 plus the actual cost of what gets bought. The fee covers the sourcing, not the materials, and there is no markup added to the receipts.

It exists because sourcing a palette properly takes a day of real work: several stores, matching ribbon widths and metal tones, and buying in the quantities a full tree needs rather than the quantities a shelf suggests. The decorations then belong to you, and they come back out of the box next year.

What is free

The estimate. It comes in writing, at no charge, because you cannot make a decision about a tree without a number in front of you. No deposit is requested until you have said yes to it.

Garlands and railings are quoted separately by the length of the run, and lobbies and offices are quoted per space after a walkthrough. Takedown after the holidays can be priced in now instead of becoming a January conversation.
