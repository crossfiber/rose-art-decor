Nobody quotes a tree sight unseen, and anyone who does is guessing. Here is the whole pricing logic in one table, so you can work out roughly where you land before you send anything.

What you provide Your ornaments, ribbon, picks and topper. We work with what is in the boxes. Nothing. You pick a color story and we handle the shopping.
What you pay on top Design and installation only. $50 flatsourcing fee, plus what the materials actually cost. No markup on the materials.
Who keeps the decor Yours already. Yours, and it comes back out of the box next year.
What sets the quote Tree height in feet, how detailed the design is, and whether it is finished all the way around or only on the exposed sides. A tree against a wall costs less than the same tree standing in the open.
Beyond the tree Garlands and railings are quoted by the length of the run. Lobbies and offices are quoted per space after a walkthrough. Takedown after the holidays can be priced in now instead of in January.
