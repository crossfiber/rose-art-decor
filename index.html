03 — How it works

Four steps, and you are done. Cuatro pasos, y listo.

You are not managing a project. You answer a few questions once, and the next time you see the tree it is finished.

01

Tell us about the tree

Height in feet, which room it lives in, whether it stands in the open or against a wall, and the colours you keep coming back to.

02

We design the look

You get a colour story and a written estimate — ribbon, florals, picks, topper and how they build up the tree in layers.

03

Yours or ours

Already own your décor? We design around it. If not, we source everything for a $50 sourcing fee plus the cost of the materials.

04

We install it

The team comes to you and builds it in place — all the way around, or only the exposed sides if the tree sits against a wall.

04 — What moves the price

No surprises at the door. Sin sorpresas en la puerta.

Every tree is quoted individually, because a 6-foot tree in a corner and a 12-foot tree in a lobby are not the same job. Here is exactly what changes the number.

You own the décor

The most common way people start.

  • Design and installation only — we work with the ornaments, ribbon and picks you already have.
  • Priced on the height of the tree and how detailed the design is.
  • We will tell you honestly if a few additions would make a real difference.
Get this quoted

We source everything

Hands-off, start to finish.

  • A $50 sourcing fee, plus the actual cost of the materials — no markup games.
  • We shop the palette, so every ribbon and floral belongs to the same story.
  • Design and installation included on top.
  • The décor is yours to keep and reuse next year.
Get this quoted

Beyond the tree

Staircases, mantels, lobbies, whole interiors.

  • Garlands and railings quoted by the linear foot of the run.
  • Commercial and lobby work quoted per space after a walkthrough.
  • Multi-room and year-round seasonal packages available.
Get this quoted
